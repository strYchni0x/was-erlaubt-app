Privacy Policy

for the Android app “was erlaubt” · Last updated: October 2026

1. Controller

Florian Willnat · florian@willnat.org

2. Summary

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics or crash reporting services.
  • There is no backend server. The developer receives no data from you at all.
  • Your location is processed on the device only and is not stored.
  • Only if no offline package is installed for your area, the app downloads road data for an area of about 2 × 2 km from the internet. The map service thereby learns roughly which area you are in.

3. Location

The app needs the location permission (precise and approximate) to detect the road you are driving on and to determine your direction and speed. Location is only requested while the app is visible (including the small picture-in-picture window), never in the background. It is processed exclusively on your device and is neither stored nor transmitted to the developer.

4. Road data from the internet (Overpass API)

If no offline package is installed for your area, the app downloads the roads of a map section (a tile of about 2 × 2 km around your position and ahead in your direction of travel) from a public OpenStreetMap server. The following is transmitted:

  • the bounds of the map section (and therefore your approximate location),
  • your IP address (technically required for any internet connection),
  • the app identifier with version number (e.g. “WasErlaubt/1.0.0 (Android)”).

These servers are used, one after another if a server is unavailable:

  • overpass-api.de (Germany)
  • overpass.private.coffee (Austria)
  • overpass.kumi.systems (Austria)

Their operators process this data under their own privacy policies, typically in server logs. Downloaded road data is cached on the device for up to 7 days. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you use).

5. Offline packages (GitHub)

In the settings you can download road data for German federal states. The package list and packages are hosted on GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) and downloaded via Android’s download manager. GitHub receives your IP address and the request, but no location data. With a package installed, the app no longer contacts the Overpass API for that area. GitHub’s privacy notice: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. Data stored on the device

  • cached road data (up to 7 days),
  • downloaded offline packages,
  • settings (e.g. notice accepted, “Wi-Fi only”, automatic updates, picture-in-picture on/off).

This data does not leave the device. You can delete it at any time via “Clear storage and cache” in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Cloud backup is disabled for the app.

7. Permissions

  • Location (precise/approximate): detect road, direction and speed.
  • Internet: download road data and offline packages.

8. Google Play

If you install the app via Google Play, Google processes data under its own terms. The app itself contains no Google services, no advertising and no analytics libraries.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the developer neither receives nor stores any of your data, he usually cannot provide information about it – for data held by the services named above, please contact their operators. Questions: florian@willnat.org.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that processes other data, this policy will be updated.
